Iron Man

Iron Man , also known as Tony Stark, is a genius billionaire inventor who builds a high-tech armored suit to fight evil. Unlike many heroes, he relies on intelligence, engineering, and innovation rather than superpowers. His arc reactor-powered suits give him abilities like flight, super strength, and advanced weapons. Over time, Iron Man evolves from a self-centered industrialist into a selfless hero who plays a key role in saving the world.

Loki

Loki is the God of Mischief and one of the most complex characters in the Marvel universe. Known for his cunning, shape-shifting abilities, and magical powers, Loki often walks the line between villain and anti-hero. As the adopted brother of Thor, his actions are driven by jealousy, ambition, and a desire for recognition, but he occasionally shows moments of growth and redemption.

Spider-Man

Spider-Man is the alter ego of Peter Parker, a teenager who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider. With powers like wall-climbing, web-shooting, and enhanced agility, he protects New York City from crime. Spider-Man is loved for his relatability, as he struggles with everyday problems while living by the motto, “With great power comes great responsibility.”

Thor

Thor is the God of Thunder from Asgard, wielding the powerful hammer Mjolnir. He has immense strength, control over lightning, and the ability to travel between realms. Despite his godly nature, Thor learns humility and responsibility, becoming a true protector of both Asgard and Earth.
